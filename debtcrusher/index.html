DebtCrusher — Privacy Policy

Last updated: 7 October 2026

DebtCrusher ("the app", "we") builds a snowball or avalanche payoff plan for your debts, on your device. This policy says exactly what the app does and does not do with your data.

The short version

  • Your debts and payoff plan never leave your phone. Everything stays in app-private storage on your device and is never uploaded to us or to anyone else.
  • No account. No login, no email, no profile.
  • We never sell or rent your data, and we never share your content with anyone.
  • The app does connect to the internet — not for your content: only for crash reports, anonymous usage counts and the Google services listed below. Section "What the app sends" lists every one of them.
  • No ads. The app shows no advertising and does not use the advertising ID.

What is stored on your device only

  • The debts you enter: name, balance, interest rate and minimum payment.
  • Your settings: display currency, monthly budget, and whether the monthly payment reminder is on and on which day.

This data lives in local, app-private storage. Android's cloud backup is switched off for this app, so it is not copied to your Google account either. Uninstalling the app permanently deletes it. We have no copy and cannot recover it.

What the app sends

The release build declares these permissions: FOREGROUND_SERVICE (merged in by the Google Firebase / Play libraries; the app runs no foreground service of its own); INTERNET and ACCESS_NETWORK_STATE (network access, used only for what the table below lists); POST_NOTIFICATIONS (to show the monthly payment reminder you switch on); RECEIVE_BOOT_COMPLETED (to re-arm that reminder after the phone restarts); WAKE_LOCK (merged in by the Google Firebase / Play libraries); com.google.android.c2dm.permission.RECEIVE (merged in by the bundled Firebase Cloud Messaging library; see the table); BIND_GET_INSTALL_REFERRER_SERVICE (used by Firebase Analytics to record which store the app was installed from). It does not declare the advertising-ID permission. Several of these are merged in by the Google libraries the app bundles rather than used by the app itself.

Third party What it receives Why
Google Firebase Crashlytics Crash stack traces, device model, OS version, app version So we can fix crashes
Google Firebase Analytics Event names (app_open, debt_added, plan_viewed), with only how many debts you have and which strategy (snowball or avalanche) you viewed — never names or amounts; plus what Firebase records automatically for every app: an app-instance ID that resets when you reinstall, device model, OS version, app version and an approximate country derived from your IP address So we can tell which features are used
Google Firebase Cloud Messaging Nothing: the library ships inside a shared notifications component, but its automatic start-up is switched off in this app, so it does not register a push token The app sends no push messages; the payment reminder is scheduled on your device

No debt name, balance, rate or payment ever appears in any of these.

Crash reports and analytics are processed by Google on our behalf under Google's privacy policy.

Advertising

The app shows no ads. An advertising library is bundled with the code but is switched off in this release: no ad is requested and no ad is shown. The app's manifest removes the advertising-ID permission (com.google.android.gms.permission.AD_ID) and the Android Ad Services permissions, and Firebase Analytics is configured not to collect the advertising ID. If we ever turn ads on, we will update this policy and the Play Data safety form before the release that does it.

Children

DebtCrusher is not directed to children under 13 and we do not knowingly collect data from them. It is a planning calculator, not financial advice.

Your rights

Everything you create is stored only on your device — delete a debt in the app at any time (swipe it away), turn the reminder off in Settings, or uninstall to erase everything. For the crash and analytics data listed above, Google acts as the processor — see Google's privacy policy for access and deletion requests.

Changes

We may update this policy; the "last updated" date will change accordingly.
