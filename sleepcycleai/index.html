SleepCycleAI — Privacy Policy

Last updated: 9 October 2026

SleepCycleAI ("the app", "we") is built privacy-first: your sleep log stays on your device. This policy says exactly what the app does and does not do with your data, including the parts that are less flattering.

The short version

  • Your sleep sessions and history never leave your phone. They are stored in app-private storage on your device and are never uploaded to us or to anyone else.
  • We do not require an account. No login, no email, no profile.
  • We never sell or rent your data, and we never share your sleep log with anyone.
  • The app does connect to the internet. Not for your sleep data — for crash reports, anonymous usage counts and purchases. Section "What the app sends" lists every one of them.

What is stored on your device only (never transmitted)

  • Your sleep sessions and their computed quality scores and cycle estimates.
  • Your alarm settings, alarm window and sound preferences.

This data lives in local, app-private storage. Uninstalling the app permanently deletes it. We have no copy and cannot recover it.

How it is stored, precisely. Your sleep sessions are kept in an ordinary plaintext SQLite database (sleepcycleai.db) inside the app's private storage directory. It is not encrypted. It is protected by Android's app sandbox — which keeps other apps out — but anyone who can read the device's storage (a rooted phone, an ADB backup, or someone holding your unlocked phone) could read it. SleepCycleAI also has no app lock or PIN. We would rather be precise than reassuring: if you need your sleep log to be encrypted at rest, this release does not do that.

What the app sends

The release manifest declares INTERNET and ACCESS_NETWORK_STATE; com.android.vending.BILLING and com.google.android.finsky.permission.BIND_GET_INSTALL_REFERRER_SERVICE for purchases; and com.google.android.c2dm.permission.RECEIVE for push transport. We do not declare any of them ourselves — every one arrives by manifest merge from the libraries below. Here is each one and what it is for:

Third party What it receives Why
Google Firebase Crashlytics Crash stack traces, device model, OS version So we can fix crashes
Google Firebase Analytics Event names — app_open, alarm_set, session_logged — with nothing attached. The upgrade (PRO) screen's events also carry where it was opened, the plan picked, its price and currency, and why a purchase failed So we can tell which features are used and whether the upgrade screen works
Google Firebase Cloud Messaging A device messaging token Bundled with the Firebase libraries; we send no push campaigns in this release
Google Play Billing Your purchase So PRO can be sold
RevenueCat Your purchase / entitlement status (e.g. "premium active") and an anonymous app user ID So PRO follows you to a new device
Google Mobile Ads SDK Nothing The SDK is bundled but ads are switched off in this release: no ad is requested or shown, and the app does not use the advertising ID

No bedtime, wake time, quality score or sleep session ever appears in any of these. The app's own analytics events are bare names, and an automated test in our build (AnalyticsSafetyTest) fails the build if anything is ever attached to them. No log line in the app prints your sleep data either.

We never receive your payment card details; those are handled solely by Google.

Alarms and notifications

The smart alarm needs to schedule an exact alarm, hold a wake lock so it can ring, restart itself after your phone reboots, post a notification when it fires and show the ringing screen over the lock screen. That is why the app requests the exact-alarm, wake-lock, boot-completed, notification and full-screen-notification permissions. So that the alarm also survives a restart during the night before you unlock the phone, the time of the next alarm (only that time — no sleep data) is kept in the part of the app's private storage that is readable before the first unlock. Sleep sounds keep playing with the screen off through a media-playback foreground service, which shows a "sleep sound is playing" notification while it runs. Nothing about the alarm or the sounds is sent anywhere.

Advertising

The Google Mobile Ads SDK ships inside the app but advertising is switched off in this release, so no ad is requested and no ad is shown. The app does not use the advertising ID: the advertising-ID permissions the bundled libraries would add are removed from the app, and Firebase Analytics is configured not to collect the advertising ID or send ad-personalization signals. If we ever turn ads on, we will update this policy and the Play Data safety form before the release that does it.

Children

SleepCycleAI is not directed to children under 13 and we do not knowingly collect data from them.

Your rights

Your sleep data is stored only on your device. You can delete any night in the app (History, then Delete on that night; there is no undo). Nights cannot be edited. Uninstalling the app (or clearing its storage in Android Settings) erases everything; we hold no copy. For the crash and analytics data listed above, Google acts as the processor — see Google's privacy policy for access and deletion requests.
