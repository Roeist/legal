Not a medical device

SleepCycleAI is a self-tracking tool for general wellbeing. Its sleep cycles, stages and quality scores are estimates calculated arithmetically from the two times you tap; the app does not measure your sleep (no microphone, no motion sensors) and the estimates can be wrong.

  • SleepCycleAI is not a medical device and does not diagnose, treat or prevent any condition, including insomnia, sleep apnoea or any other sleep disorder.

The alarm

The smart alarm is meant to wake you inside the window you choose, but it depends on your phone: it can only ring when notifications and exact alarms are allowed for the app, the phone is switched on and charged, and the sound is not muted by the system. Do not rely on SleepCycleAI as your only alarm when waking up on time really matters (a flight, a shift, medication).
