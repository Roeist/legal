Your diary

Your entries (the daily 1–10 rating, your notes and your tags) belong to you. They are stored only on your device, in an encrypted database; we have no copy and cannot recover them. You can delete any day's entry in the app (Timeline, open the day, Delete entry; there is no undo). If you uninstall the app or lose the device, your entries are gone: the app has no cloud sync and no account. "Share encrypted diary file" in Settings is not a backup — only the install that made the file holds its key, so nothing (not MoodTrack, not us) can restore it.

The app lock uses your phone's own screen lock (fingerprint, face or device PIN). It protects the screen, not the phone: anyone who knows your device PIN can open it.

MoodTrack is a personal journaling and self-tracking tool for noticing patterns in how your days feel. Its averages, trend and per-tag charts are simple arithmetic on the numbers you enter.
