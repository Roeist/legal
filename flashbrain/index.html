FlashBrain — Privacy Policy

Last updated: 9 October 2026

FlashBrain ("the app", "we") is built privacy-first: your decks and cards stay on your device. This policy says exactly what the app does and does not do with your data, including the parts that are less flattering.

The short version

  • Your decks, cards and review history never leave your phone. They are stored in app-private storage on your device and are never uploaded to us or to anyone else.
  • We do not require an account. No login, no email, no profile.
  • We never sell or rent your data, and we never share your card content with anyone.
  • The app does connect to the internet. Not for your cards — for crash reports, anonymous usage counts and purchases. Section "What the app sends" lists every one of them.

What is stored on your device only (never transmitted)

  • Your decks, your cards, and the spaced-repetition schedule for each card.
  • Your settings.

This data lives in local, app-private storage. Uninstalling the app permanently deletes it. We have no copy and cannot recover it. Because there is no account, your decks do not follow you to another device — export a CSV if you want to move them.

A CSV you export leaves the app only through your device's share sheet, to whichever app or person you choose. We never see it.

What the app sends

The release manifest declares INTERNET and ACCESS_NETWORK_STATE; com.android.vending.BILLING and com.google.android.finsky.permission.BIND_GET_INSTALL_REFERRER_SERVICE for purchases; and WAKE_LOCK and FOREGROUND_SERVICE, which the Google Play and Firebase libraries merge in — we run no foreground service of our own. We declare none of these ourselves; every one arrives by manifest merge from the libraries below. The advertising-ID permissions those libraries would also add (AD_ID and the Android Ad Services permissions) are removed from the app, and Firebase is told not to collect the advertising ID. Here is each third party and what it receives:

Third party What it receives Why
Google Firebase Crashlytics Crash stack traces, device model, OS version So we can fix crashes
Google Firebase Analytics Usage events and a few non-identifying details: app_open; deck_created; review_complete with the number of cards the session reviewed; and for the supporter tip paywall_shown / paywall_dismissed (where it was offered), paywall_cta_tapped / checkout_started (the product id), purchase_succeeded (product id, price, currency), purchase_failed (product id, a failure code), purchase_restored and feature_used (a Restore tap). Firebase also records its own app-instance identifier So we can tell which parts of the app are used and whether the tip works
Google Play Billing Your purchase So the one-time supporter tip can be sold
RevenueCat Your purchase status and an anonymous app user ID So the supporter tip is restorable on a new device
Google Mobile Ads SDK Nothing — it is bundled but switched off in this release; no ad is requested or shown —

No deck name, card front, card back or review rating ever appears in any of these. The only values attached to analytics events are the ones listed above.

We never receive your payment card details; those are handled solely by Google.

Advertising

The Google Mobile Ads SDK ships inside the app but advertising is switched off in this release, so no ad is requested and no ad is shown. The app does not use your advertising ID: the permission is removed from the app and Firebase's advertising-ID collection is turned off. If we ever turn ads on, we will update this policy and the Play Data safety form before the release that does it.

Children

FlashBrain is not directed to children under 13 and we do not knowingly collect data from them.

Your rights

Your study data is stored only on your device — delete decks in-app at any time, or uninstall to erase everything. For the crash and analytics data listed above, Google acts as the processor — see Google's privacy policy for access and deletion requests.

Changes

We may update this policy; the "last updated" date will change accordingly.
