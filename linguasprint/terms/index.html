The course

LinguaSprint is a short Spanish course for beginners: six modules, 12 short lessons. The lessons are written in English. It is a learning aid; we do not promise any level of fluency, exam result or certificate. Pronunciation is spoken by your device's own text-to-speech engine, so how it sounds depends on that engine.

Your progress (finished lessons, scores, XP and level) is stored only on your device. We have no copy and cannot recover it: uninstalling the app or clearing its storage deletes it, and there is no account and no sync between devices.
