CycleLuna — Privacy Policy

Last updated: 9 October 2026

CycleLuna ("the app", "we") is built privacy-first: your cycle information stays on your device. This policy says exactly what the app does and does not do with your data, including the parts that are less flattering.

The short version

  • Your cycle and symptom data never leaves your phone. It is stored in app-private storage on your device and is never uploaded to us or to anyone else.
  • It is not encrypted on disk. We say this plainly because a period tracker that implies encryption it does not have is worse than one that admits it. See "How it is stored, precisely".
  • We do not require an account. No login, no email, no profile.
  • We never sell or rent your data, and we never share your cycle entries with anyone.
  • The app does connect to the internet. Not for your cycle data — for crash reports, anonymous usage counts and purchases. Section "What the app sends" lists every one of them.

What is stored on your device only (never transmitted)

  • Your logged periods, cycle history, flow, symptoms, moods and notes.
  • Your forecast and your settings.

This data lives in local, app-private storage. Uninstalling the app permanently deletes it. We have no copy and cannot recover it.

How it is stored, precisely. Your cycle entries are kept in an ordinary plaintext SQLite database (cycleluna.db) inside the app's private storage directory. It is not encrypted at rest — the data is not cryptographically scrambled on disk. It is protected by Android's app sandbox, which keeps other apps out, and, if you turn it on, by the app lock (fingerprint / face / device PIN). The app lock gates the screen, not the file: it does not encrypt the database. Anyone who can read the device's storage — a rooted phone, an ADB backup, or someone holding your unlocked phone — could read your cycle history.

We would rather be precise than reassuring. If encryption at rest is a requirement for you, this release does not provide it, and we will not claim otherwise.

What the app sends

The merged manifest declares INTERNET and ACCESS_NETWORK_STATE; the advertising ID permission com.google.android.gms.permission.AD_ID; the three Android Ad Services permissions ACCESS_ADSERVICES_AD_ID, ACCESS_ADSERVICES_ATTRIBUTION and ACCESS_ADSERVICES_TOPICS; com.android.vending.BILLING and com.google.android.finsky.permission.BIND_GET_INSTALL_REFERRER_SERVICE for purchases; and WAKE_LOCK and FOREGROUND_SERVICE, which the Google Play and Firebase libraries merge in — we run no foreground service of our own. The only permission we declare ourselves is USE_BIOMETRIC (plus USE_FINGERPRINT, added by the AndroidX biometric library for older Android versions) for the app lock. Everything else arrives by manifest merge from the libraries below. Here is each one and what it is for:

Third party What it receives Why
Google Firebase Crashlytics Crash stack traces, device model, OS version So we can fix crashes
Google Firebase Analytics Event names such as app_open, cycle_logged and insights_viewed with no parameters; and, from the Premium screen only, purchase-funnel events (paywall_shown, purchase_succeeded, ...) that carry the screen name, the product id of the plan, the price and currency of a purchase, or the reason a purchase failed. Firebase also attaches its own app-instance identifier So we can tell which screens are used and whether Premium works
Google Play Billing Your purchase So Premium can be sold
RevenueCat Your purchase / entitlement status (e.g. "premium active") and an anonymous app user ID So Premium follows you to a new device
Google Mobile Ads SDK Advertising ID availability The SDK is bundled; ads are switched off in this release and no ad is requested or shown

No cycle data, symptom, note, date or mood ever appears in any of these. CycleLuna's own events have no parameters, and an automated test in our build (AnalyticsSafetyTest) fails if one of them ever gains a parameter; the purchase-funnel events carry only the purchase details listed above. No log line in the app prints your health data either.

We never receive your payment card details; those are handled solely by Google.

Advertising

The Google Mobile Ads SDK ships inside the app but advertising is switched off in this release, so no ad is requested and no ad is shown. Because the SDK is present, Android still lists the advertising-ID permission for the app. If we ever turn ads on, we will update this policy and the Play Data safety form before the release that does it.

Children

CycleLuna is not directed to children under 13 and we do not knowingly collect data from them.

Your rights

Your cycle data is stored only on your device, so you are in full control: edit or delete entries in-app at any time, or uninstall to erase everything. For the crash and analytics data listed above, Google acts as the processor — see Google's privacy policy for access and deletion requests.
